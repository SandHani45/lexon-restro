# Brag Plan: EasyBillBro

## What is this app?
EasyBillBro is a cloud POS platform for Indian restaurants that turns a photographed menu into a fully working digital menu and billing system in under a minute.

## The angle
The whole pitch collapses into one demonstrable moment: point a phone at a handwritten or printed menu, and EasyBillBro builds the real thing — items, prices, categories — while you watch. The video's job is to let that moment actually happen on screen, framed by the premium gold-on-black identity the product already uses to differentiate itself from dated Indian POS software.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The hero line from the site, full scale on black: "Your restaurant. *Finally* running like it should." The word "Finally" lands in gold italic a beat after the rest — the one flourish before the product takes over.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The AI menu import card recreated from the site: a photographed menu turns into live menu items — name + gold price — arriving one by one while a mono countdown ticks up, landing under a minute.
- One sharp, held claim: no annual contract, cancel anytime — the thing every Indian restaurant owner has been burned by with other POS vendors.

## Outro / punchline
The gold diamond mark and "EasyBillBro" wordmark settle on black, then one line underneath: "₹999/month. No contract. Built in Bengaluru." Hold. No CTA button — the product just sits there, confident.

## User flow worth showing
The site's AI Menu Import section is the one place this landing page shows the product *doing* something rather than describing it: photo in → items populate one at a time with prices → countdown settles under 60s. That's the centerpiece. (This is a marketing-page product demo, not an authenticated in-app flow — the deeper POS screens like the floor plan and KOT system aren't available as static markup to recreate faithfully, so the AI import moment carries the "product in use" weight alone.)

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium boutique reveal — gold-on-black, restrained motion, founder-led confidence. Not a toy app, not a hype reel.
- Interpretation: slow, confident holds over quick cuts; one flourish (the gold "Finally," the countdown) rather than many; type stays mixed-case with generous spacing; nothing shouts.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 19s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0a0a0a (black), panel #161616
- Accent: #c5a059 (gold), gold-light #e8d5a3
- Text: #ffffff (headlines), rgba(255,255,255,0.5) (secondary)

- Body font: Outfit
- Mono accent (prices, timer): Space Mono
- Strongest visual element: the AI Menu Import card — black panel, traffic-light dots, menu rows sliding in with gold Space-Mono prices, gold countdown timer beneath

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 5s
Black background with the faint gold hairline grid from the site (barely visible, no motion). "Your restaurant." fades up first (DM Serif Display, white), then "Finally" arrives half a beat later in gold italic, then "running like it should." in muted white completes the line. Voice narration plays under this, not reading the line verbatim.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the three line fragments arrive in sequence (white → gold italic → muted white), not simultaneously.
Audio intent: quiet confidence, no urgency — this is the calm before the product shows itself.
Audio-coupled idea: none (let the type arrive silently against the bed; no SFX on this scene, per polished restraint).
Narration line: "Every restaurant deserves software that actually works."
Music: bed enters here, ~0.30 volume, no swell.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — AI Menu Import (the flow) — 7s
Recreate the site's AI Menu Import card centered on black: dark panel, three status dots, "AI MENU IMPORT" label in gold micro-caps. Below it, menu rows (Paneer Tikka ₹280, Garlic Naan ₹55, Dal Makhani ₹180, Mango Lassi ₹120, Chicken Biryani ₹320, Masala Chai ₹60) arrive one at a time, each sliding in from the left with its gold Space-Mono price. A mono countdown beneath ticks up in sync and settles on a value under 60s once all six rows have landed.
Sequential/interaction: yes — six menu rows arrive one by one in this exact order, timed to the countdown; this is the product's flagship action, simulated as it would actually run.
Audio intent: quiet build, satisfaction on settle — this is the proof, not a pitch.
Audio-coupled idea: soft accent on the first row's arrival only (not all six); a slightly more present accent when the countdown settles, marking completion.
Narration line: "Photograph your menu. EasyBillBro reads it, and builds it — in under a minute."
Music: bed continues, steady, unchanged volume.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Highlight: no annual contract — 3s
The AI card dissolves. One held line, large, centered: "No annual contract." in white, then "Cancel anytime." arrives just after in gold, smaller, beneath it. Plenty of black space around the words — the polished restraint move.
Sequential/interaction: none (two lines, staggered arrival, then both hold).
Audio intent: a quiet exhale — the reassurance beat after the technical proof.
Audio-coupled idea: none — let this scene sit in near-silence against the bed.
Narration line: "Cancel anytime. Message the founder directly."
Music: bed continues, unchanged.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 4s
The gold diamond mark (rotated square with a black inset, from the site's nav logo) settles center-frame, then "EasyBillBro" in DM Serif Display beneath it. A final line fades in small and muted: "₹999/month. No contract. Built in Bengaluru." Hold to black.
Sequential/interaction: yes — mark settles first, wordmark follows, tagline last; then a still hold.
Audio intent: settled, confident close — the one moment the video allows itself a small landing accent.
Audio-coupled idea: single soft accent as the mark settles.
Narration line: "EasyBillBro. Built for Indian restaurants."
Music: bed fades down through the hold, out by the final frame.
Transition mood: hold → end

**Music mood for this video:** polished — steady, warm, business-clean, never overtakes the voice or the type.
**Audio summary:** One steady bed under the whole video at low volume, three quiet SFX accents (first menu row, countdown settle, logo settle), and four short narration lines that complement rather than read the on-screen copy — the audio stays in the background so the gold-on-black visuals and the AI import proof carry the video.
