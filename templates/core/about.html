About EasyBillBro

EasyBillBro started with a simple complaint restaurant owners kept repeating: their POS software was sold with an annual contract, then handed off to a support queue. Here's why that's not how we built this one.

Why EasyBillBro exists
GST-compliant software, without the enterprise runaround.

Most restaurant POS software sold in India asks for an annual commitment before an owner has even tried it. Support means a ticket number and a wait. EasyBillBro was built to remove both of those, starting with the basics every Indian restaurant actually needs: CGST/SGST-correct billing, GSTIN and FSSAI on every bill, a live floor plan the whole floor staff can see, and kitchen order tickets that don't duplicate under pressure.

The AI menu import — turning a photo of a handwritten or printed menu into a full digital menu in under 60 seconds — exists because manual data entry was the single biggest reason restaurant owners kept putting off switching software at all.
