Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does EasyBillBro cost? Are there hidden fees? ▼
EasyBillBro starts at ₹999/month for 1 outlet (Solo Plan) and ₹2,499/month for up to 3 outlets (Growth Plan). There are zero setup fees, zero hidden maintenance charges, and no annual contracts. You can cancel at any time with a single click.
Is EasyBillBro fully GST compliant in India? ▼
Yes, 100%. EasyBillBro prints your GSTIN and FSSAI license numbers on all thermal bills and WhatsApp receipts, breaks down CGST and SGST per item (5%, 12%, 18%), and generates one-click CSV reports ready for your CA to file GSTR-1.
How does the 60-second AI menu import work? ▼
Simply snap a phone picture of your physical menu card, printed sheet, or handwritten list. Our AI reads the text, automatically extracts dish names, prices, categories, and veg/non-veg markers, and creates your menu ready to bill in under 60 seconds.
Can I run EasyBillBro on my existing phones or desktop? ▼
Yes! EasyBillBro runs in any modern web browser on Windows, Mac, iPads, Android tablets, and smartphones. You do not need to purchase any proprietary terminal hardware.
