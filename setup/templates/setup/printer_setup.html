How EasyBillBro Prints Receipts & KOTs

Because this cloud POS runs on the web, it cannot directly reach printers inside your local restaurant Wi-Fi router. The EasyBillBro Phone App (or PC background agent) runs on a device connected to the same Wi-Fi network as your thermal printer. When a bill or KOT is punched, the phone app pulls the job from the cloud and transmits raw ESC/POS commands to the printer IP address within 1–2 seconds.

Printer IP Address

The local network IP address assigned to your thermal printer

Local Network
How to find your printer IP: Turn off your thermal printer, hold down the FEED button, and turn the power back on. Keep holding FEED for 3 seconds until a self-test slip prints out. Look for the line that says IP Address: 192.168.x.x.
