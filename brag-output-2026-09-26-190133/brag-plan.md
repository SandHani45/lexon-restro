# Brag Plan: EasyBillBro — "Billing so simple, anyone can do it"

## What is this app?
EasyBillBro is a GST billing + restaurant POS that runs on any Android phone: tap a dish, tap bill, print — with tables, kitchen tickets and daily earnings in the same app, from ₹999/month with no setup fee and no contract.

## The angle
Not "enterprise POS". This is for the dhaba owner, the small hotel, the counter where the cashier may not read English well. The whole film argues one thing: **if you can use WhatsApp, you can use EasyBillBro.** Big buttons, pictures of food, colours instead of words (green = free table), and a price smaller than a daily chai bill (~₹33/day).

## Hook (0–5s)
A handwritten bill-pad on dark: crossed-out totals, "Still writing bills by hand?" — then the paper slides away and a phone rises in its place. The problem is instantly recognisable to every dhaba owner.

## Key moments
- **Tap. Tap. Bill.** The real POS screen (screenshot 3): a finger-tap ripple hits "+ Paneer Tikka", then "+ Dal Makhani", then "Bill & Print". Three giant step numbers 1–2–3 alongside. This is the centerpiece.
- **Colours, not words.** Phone floor-plan screens (8, 9): green = free, orange = busy.
- **Kitchen gets it instantly.** Orders screen (4) → Kitchen Display (5): "No shouting. No lost slips."
- **Know your money every night.** Dashboard (2) with ₹3,284 today's revenue counting up; phone order history (10) with cash/UPI breakdown.
- **The price.** ₹999/month → "≈ ₹33 a day". ₹0 setup · No contract · Any Android phone.

## Outro
Logo on black, "Simple billing for every dhaba, hotel & restaurant." + easybillbro.in · Free demo.

## User flow worth showing
Open POS → tap dishes → Bill & Print → order appears for kitchen → revenue shows on dashboard at day end. All from the user's real screenshots.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store (with polished restraint)
- Creative direction: warm, trustworthy, "made for Bharat" product film — premium look, but the words are simple enough for a first-time smartphone user.
- Interpretation: short on-screen words (2–5 per line), big type, slow-enough holds; every scene shows the real app; smooth slides/zooms, no chaos.
- Duration override: user asked for **1 minute** (60s) — overrides the 15–25s default.
